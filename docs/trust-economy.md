# World Makers Trust Economy

World Makers may monetize products and family services, but it must never monetize a child's vulnerability to pressure, randomness, social comparison, loss aversion, or confusion about money.

## Product principles

1. **Parent-owned commerce.** Children can discover and save interests; only an authenticated parent/guardian can enter a paid flow.
2. **Transparent value.** Paid products disclose what is included. No mystery purchases or hidden conversion rates.
3. **No purchasable premium currency.** Gameplay currencies/rewards, if any, are earned only through play and cannot buy paid catalog items.
4. **Deterministic rewards.** Paid value is never randomized. Gameplay rewards are defined by explicit triggers and outcomes.
5. **No attention extraction.** No lost streaks, expiring reward tracks, energy timers, nag loops, or fake scarcity.
6. **No third-party ads in child gameplay.** This is a World Makers product rule even where a platform might allow restricted advertising.
7. **Progress survives commercial changes.** Subscription cancellation/refund/revocation may remove future access to paid content where legally/platform-appropriate, but never deletes child worlds, achievements, learning evidence, or base progress.
8. **Right to Stop.** Natural session exits are a success condition, not a failure to retain.

## Architecture

```text
Child Runtime
  |-- Progression / Rewards (earned, deterministic)
  |-- Entitlement read (authorized, read-only)
  `-- Save for Family / Interest request (optional, rate-limited)

                     X  no payment edge

Parent Portal / Parent Trust Zone
  |-- Parent authentication
  |-- Commerce catalog + localized price
  |-- Parent authorization
  |-- Platform/provider checkout adapter
  |-- Server transaction verification
  |-- Entitlement grant/revoke/restore
  `-- Purchase/subscription history
```

## Allowed paid product types

- base/premium game purchase;
- biome/expansion pack;
- creative/building pack;
- cosmetic pack with no power advantage;
- optional family subscription/membership;
- institutional/education licensing.

Consumable paid currency is not an allowed product type.

## Reward model

Approved reward types are:

- `creative_unlock`;
- `tool_unlock`;
- `mastery_badge`;
- `narrative_unlock`;
- `discovery`.

Every gameplay reward must be `deterministic: true`, `purchasable: false`, `transferable: false`, and `convertibleToMoney: false`.

## Interest requests

A child-facing offer may expose a neutral `saveForFamily` action when family settings allow it.

Rules:

- no checkout, price confirmation, payment credentials, or receipt handling in child runtime;
- no wording that pressures the child to ask a parent;
- no urgency or countdown;
- no repeated push/email notification generated by the child action;
- server-side rate limit/cooldown;
- parent may disable the feature entirely;
- ignoring/declining an interest request must not change gameplay or child-facing feedback in a punitive way.

## Prohibited patterns

The following are architectural violations:

- loot boxes, gacha, paid random rewards, mystery purchases;
- purchasable premium gems/coins/tokens or obscured price conversion;
- pay-to-win or paid learning boosts;
- rewarded ads, ad walls, third-party ads in child gameplay;
- daily login loss-aversion loops;
- streak reset/punishment for absence;
- expiring battle passes or paid FOMO tracks;
- pay-to-skip friction deliberately introduced for monetization;
- energy/life systems refillable with money;
- fake scarcity, child-facing countdown sales, emotional pressure, or repeated nagging;
- public wealth or spending status among children;
- commercial push notifications targeting child profiles;
- payment SDKs or receipt-validation code linked into child runtime.

## Healthy product metrics

Business metrics may be observed, but product optimization should prioritize creative completion/diversity, learning mastery, safe social sessions, parent trust, crash-free sessions, healthy exit rate, and purchase satisfaction.

`session_length`, `DAU`, `retention`, `conversion`, and `ARPU` are not license to introduce coercive retention mechanics.

## Reviews required before commerce implementation

Before production checkout is enabled, require:

- trust/safety review;
- child UX review;
- privacy review;
- legal/compliance review for target markets;
- platform-specific App Store / Google Play / console policy review;
- refund/restore/parent-support operational review;
- threat model for parental authorization and entitlement forgery.

See `docs/compliance/child-commerce-boundaries.md` and `services/backend/contracts/commerce.openapi.yaml`.
