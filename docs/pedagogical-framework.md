# Pedagogical Framework / Marco pedagógico

> Draft framework for educator review. Learning should emerge through meaningful game mechanics, not merely through quizzes attached to play.

## Design principles

1. **Learning by doing** — objectives are expressed through building, observing, comparing, planning, explaining, or choosing.
2. **Visible evidence** — each mission defines what observable action counts as evidence of understanding.
3. **Age-band calibration** — complexity, reading load, number sense, and motor demands differ across 4–6, 7–8, and 9–10.
4. **Non-punitive feedback** — mistakes should encourage iteration rather than loss or shame.
5. **Transfer** — missions should connect concepts to new constructions, environments, stories, or decisions.
6. **Privacy minimization** — evidence records should use objective IDs and pseudonymous profile IDs, not sensitive narrative profiles of children.

## Axis 1 — Mathematics / Matemáticas

Potential concepts:

- measurement and comparison;
- proportion and scale;
- spatial reasoning and geometry;
- patterns and sequences;
- resource budgeting and arithmetic;
- estimation.

Mechanic examples: build a bridge to a measured span; allocate a fixed material budget; resize decorative patterns proportionally.

## Axis 2 — Science / Ciencias

Potential concepts:

- ecosystems and interdependence;
- plant/water cycles;
- weather and environment;
- materials and properties;
- observation, prediction, cause/effect.

Mechanic examples: restore habitat conditions, observe ecosystem response, design shelter/material choices for environmental constraints.

**Pedagogue placeholders**

- [ ] Inquiry progression.
- [ ] Vocabulary bands.
- [ ] Evidence rubric.

## Axis 3 — Language & Narrative / Lengua y narrativa

Potential concepts:

- sequence and causality;
- comprehension;
- vocabulary in context;
- dialogue and perspective;
- oral/written storytelling;
- branching narrative consequences.

Mechanic examples: reconstruct story order from environmental clues; choose dialogue based on inferred character goals; build a setting and narrate its story.

**Pedagogue placeholders**

- [ ] Reading-level policy.
- [ ] Localization/read-aloud strategy.
- [ ] Evidence rubric.

## Axis 4 — History & Culture / Historia y cultura

Real cultures must be represented with context, provenance, and sensitivity—not as decorative stereotypes.

Potential concepts:

- material culture and everyday life;
- environment and settlement;
- architecture and technology;
- chronology/change;
- multiple perspectives;
- cultural continuity and diversity.

Mechanic examples: investigate why a settlement uses particular materials; compare building responses to climate; encounter curated stories tied to place and time.

**Pedagogue/content placeholders**

- [ ] Source/provenance requirements.
- [ ] Community/expert consultation threshold.
- [ ] Sensitive-topic review procedure.
- [ ] Evidence rubric.

## Mission metadata minimum

Every pedagogical mission should define:

- stable mission ID;
- curriculum axis;
- target age band;
- learning objective IDs;
- player-facing goal;
- gameplay mechanic mapping;
- success/evidence events;
- scaffolds/hints;
- difficulty;
- localization keys;
- pedagogy review status;
- cultural review status when applicable;
- analytics/privacy classification.

See `content/missions/schema/mission.schema.json`.
